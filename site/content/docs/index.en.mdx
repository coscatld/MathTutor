## Three places, three purposes

**Math conversation: work through a problem.** Make an attempt, ask for a hint when needed, then check your understanding on a new problem. See the [tutoring workflow](user/tutoring-workflow).

**Knowledge library: organize reference material.** Store notes and question packs, choose the sources in a conversation, and follow citations back to the text. Importing a document does not establish learning evidence.

**Learning profile: review evidence from actual attempts.** Independent work, assisted work, and attempts after seeing a solution are recorded separately. Use those records to choose your next practice. Model predictions and self-reports cannot replace independent checks.

## Versions and evidence

The current release is **0.7.1**, with recent fixes for graphs, drafts, learning evidence, and document handling. See [installation](user/installation) for files and platform acceptance records.

Implemented features have corresponding repository code; partial and experimental features state their limits. Engineering tests, fixture evaluations, and real student learning outcomes are assessed separately. DKT training and synthetic evaluations are not classroom evidence.
