## Why does macOS say the app is damaged?

Use the r3 DMG. The original build was withdrawn. r3 is still not notarized, so you may need to allow it. See [Installation](../installation).

## Why are some items not step-checked?

Verification covers supported algebraic families. Proofs and out-of-grammar items take an unverified or unsupported path.

## Does DKT prove learning gains?

No. The weights come from a research pipeline on public ASSISTments data. Learning effect on MathTutor students is unverified.

## Is there a Linux installer?

There is no target-machine-verified public package.

## How does MathTutor relate to DeepTutor?

MathTutor is a standalone product extracted from DeepTutor’s math-tutoring work, licensed Apache-2.0. The copyright notice still includes Data Intelligence Lab, The University of Hong Kong. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` for other attributions.
