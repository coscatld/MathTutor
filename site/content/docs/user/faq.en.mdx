## What if my system prompts me on first launch?

Check the downloaded file and checksum against the official release page. See [installation](./installation.mdx) for platform-specific steps.

## How do I see Zhihu sources?

Connect Zhihu under Knowledge sources and enable Online in chat. Open a citation below an answer to read the excerpt and original. [Knowledge exploration](./knowledge-exploration.mdx)

## Why does MathTutor ask for more information?

Several answers can fit the same description. For example, a rectangle’s perimeter alone does not determine its area. Add the missing information to continue. The tutor identifies questions beyond its local verification scope.

## How is work after a hint recorded?

Independent attempts and work after hints or a solution are recorded separately. A fresh exercise lets you check whether you can solve a similar problem independently. [Learning evidence](./learning-evidence.mdx)
