title: Math Tutoring Workflow
description: How independent attempts, graded hints, disclosure, and transfer practice connect.
---

1. Enter a problem and try a first step yourself. Add missing information if the tutor asks for it.
2. Request a hint, an explanation or an example when you need it. You can view the solution after a hint on an unfinished problem.
3. Accept a related exercise to check whether you can solve it independently. Your profile records how much help you used on each attempt.

## Check a derivation

When solving `2x+3=7`, write one equation per line. The checker accepts `2x=4` as an equivalent intermediate step and `x=2` as the solution. If you write `2x=10` and then the correct final answer, the feedback identifies the first line that changed the solution set. A step the verifier cannot establish, such as cancellation of a variable denominator, remains open for clarification.

The ∑ menu inserts fractions, powers, parentheses, inequality signs and new lines. While writing an attempt, you can paste a scratch image and submit the text you entered for checking. Select “Ask about this image” to discuss the picture with a configured vision model.

## Concepts and graphs

Ask why moving a term across an equation changes its sign to discuss a concept. Ask to draw y = x² − 2x − 3 to see the graph and its coordinates. Using hints or a graph for your current problem is recorded as assistance.
