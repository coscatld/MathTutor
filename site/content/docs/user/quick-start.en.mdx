---
title: First steps
description: Try a problem, use a hint and check your understanding with a new exercise.
---

1. [Install](./installation.mdx) and open MathTutor. You may skip initial model setup and assessment. Choose your interface language in Settings.
2. In a new conversation, enter `2x + 3 = 7`. Try writing your own first step.
3. If you get stuck, request a hint and try again. After a hint you can reveal the worked solution.
4. When offered a related exercise, try it on your own. Open your learning profile to see how much help you used on each problem.

Local math practice works offline. Open Knowledge Exploration to search encyclopedias and textbooks, or choose local materials to search your own lecture notes. [Knowledge exploration](./knowledge-exploration.mdx)
