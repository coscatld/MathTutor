There is no `class EvidenceTracker`. The production policy string is **`evidence_tracker_v2`**, implemented across:

- `learning/evidence.py` — evidence factory
- `learning/knowledge_tracing/service.py` — `StudentStateService`
- `learning/knowledge_tracing/events.py` — events and baseline prediction

DKT:

- Runtime `LSTMTracker` plus an optional source-model package
- `pedagogy/dkt_policy.py`: at most one notch, and only when `controls_teaching` is set, the skill maps, independent target history exists, and the probability is in [0, 1]
- Bundled weights: numeric derivatives of ASSISTments 2009–2010 skill builder, with no raw student rows. `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` records that redistribution terms for derived weights are **not yet confirmed**

Do not describe synthetic training, shadow forecasts, or ASSISTments fit as MathTutor classroom outcomes.
